approved before production → produce and post → monthly report.

passwords), the client owns final files once paid. To start: "Send your brief" on muritu.top/services
(reply within 48 hours).

Prices are quoted per project after the brief; no public price list. To start: "Start a project"
on daelus.co.ke. Daelus replies within one working day "with questions, not a quote".
